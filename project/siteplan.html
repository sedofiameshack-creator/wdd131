Website Planning Document

Site Name

01

ReelTrack

The name pairs "reel," the film canister that carries every movie ever projected, with "track," the act of following what you watch and what you still want to see. Together they describe exactly what the site does: it helps someone keep a running record of films watched, rated, and queued up next.

Optional domain availability: reeltrack.app

Site Purpose

02

ReelTrack gives film fans a personal hub for managing what they watch. Visitors can browse a shared library of movies, add titles to a personal watchlist, log ratings and short reviews once they've seen something, and filter the library by genre or release year to decide what to watch next.

Scenarios

03

  • I just finished a movie and want to log my rating and a short review before I forget my thoughts — how do I do that?
  • I have twenty minutes before I need to leave — what's a short, highly rated movie I've already added to my watchlist?
  • I loved a director's last film — how can I filter the library to find similar titles by genre?

Color Schema

04

The palette borrows from a dim theater: a near-black auditorium background, a marquee gold that recalls house lights and ticket stubs, and a muted teal that reads as calm and cinematic rather than loud.

  • Cinema Black — #14110F
    Page background, footer
  • Screen Cream — #F2EDE4
    Body text, card backgrounds
  • Marquee Gold — #E8A33D
    Headings, buttons, active states
  • Velvet Teal — #2C5F5A
    Links, borders, secondary accents
